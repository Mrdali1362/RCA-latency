RCA Latency Test

We would like to test our prototype for adjusting RCA latency for speakers that are connected via RCA output to Heddon.

You will hear two sounds. Start by playing the sample, then adjust the delay based on what you hear. Tap "Confirm sync" when you think they match.

At the end of the test, we will ask you 3 simple questions to understand how and if the prototype matches what you expected it to do. Your answers will be sent to us in the app team and will be removed after this short study.

We record your delay adjustments, timings and the answers you give at the end. Results are only sent if you tap "Send results", from your own mail app, so your name and email address will be visible to us. All data will be removed at the end of the study.
